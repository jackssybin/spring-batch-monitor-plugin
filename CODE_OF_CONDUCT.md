# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful place for people who use, maintain, and contribute to Spring Batch developer tooling.

Participants are expected to communicate constructively, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Provide actionable feedback.
- Welcome new contributors and users.
- Avoid sharing private credentials, private infrastructure details, or sensitive batch metadata.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on evidence and project goals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly derailing project discussions.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors when behavior harms the project or community.

For serious concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository owner profile or the configured repository contact channel.
